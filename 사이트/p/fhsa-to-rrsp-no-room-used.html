The FHSA no-lose exit checklist

Tax & accountsFIRST HOME BOX2 min readSTEP 22026-11-05

Take your FHSA cash out and tax hits $40,000.

An FHSA is a savings account for a first home, and you can put in at most $40,000 over your whole life. If the home never happens, you can move that money straight into your RRSP or RRIF, which are retirement savings accounts. Done as a direct transfer, there is usually no tax right away, and it generally does not use up your unused RRSP room.

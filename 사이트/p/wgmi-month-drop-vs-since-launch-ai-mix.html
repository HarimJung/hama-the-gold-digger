WGMI is a fund that holds many companies at once, and it used to hold only bitcoin miners, companies whose computers make new bitcoin. On August 11, 2026, it changed what it is allowed to hold. Now AI chips, the tiny brains AI runs on, plus data centers, giant buildings full of computers that run AI, and power companies count too.

WHY IT MATTERSXIC is one fund that holds the whole Canadian stock market.

A single unit spreads your money across 217 holdings.

Because XIC owns the whole market rather than one company, the 2020 crash was a drop in all of Canada's big businesses together, and they came back together; but it also means a market-wide fall hits the fund in full, with nothing inside it to dodge the drop.

Today — Open your bank or investing app today and write down the name of the fund you hold and how many companies are inside it.

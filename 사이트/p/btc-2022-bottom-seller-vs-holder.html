WHY IT MATTERSBitcoin is digital money that no company or government runs.

Only 21 million bitcoins will ever be created.

Holders treat that fixed supply as the reason a crash can be a discount, but no company earnings sit behind the price, so it rises or falls only with demand and a drop like 2022's can happen again.

Today — Write down the one thing that would make you sell, and keep the note somewhere you'll see it next time prices drop.

WHY IT MATTERSSOXX is one basket of US chip companies, including the ones powering AI.

It holds just 30 stocks, all from one industry.

Every holding depends on the same chip cycle, so the basket has no other industry to cushion a fall, and that is why it dropped 25% or more four times in ten years; it also means a fall hits the whole sector at once instead of one broken company, which is why past drops have been bought back.

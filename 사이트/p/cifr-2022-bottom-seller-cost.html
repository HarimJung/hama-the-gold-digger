Born in fearCIFR2 min readSTEP 32026-11-06

CIFR sellers missed $600. Want it back?

Cipher Mining, called CIFR on the stock market, is a company whose computers make new bitcoin. It now also rents out giant buildings full of computers that run AI, and Amazon signed a 15-year rent deal with it worth about $5.5 billion. On December 29, 2022 its share price fell to US$0.38, the lowest it has ever been, and on November 5, 2026 it dropped about 20% in one day to around US$5.11.

Born in fearIREN3 min readSTEP 52026-11-05

IREN fell 53.4%. Rents computers to AI, 85% already signed.

IREN is a company that builds giant buildings full of computers that run AI, and rents that computing power to AI companies. In July 2026 it told the government it now aims for more than $4 billion a year from that AI renting business, and said about 85% of that amount is already covered by signed multi-year deals worth $2.8 billion. Its share price dropped 53.4% in one month, so the chart looks bad while the plan on paper looks big.

WHY IT MATTERSIREN builds AI data centers and rents computing power to AI companies.

It targets over $4 billion a year, about 85% already signed.

Signed multi-year contracts are part of why some see the sell-off as a discount, but the $4 billion is a run-rate target, not booked revenue, and it depends on 480MW being built, tested and accepted by customers on time.

Today — Write down the $4 billion target and the 480MW build, and check at IREN's next update whether the buildings are finished.

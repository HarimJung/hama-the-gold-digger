WHY IT MATTERSSOXL owns no chip company outright. It's a bet on the 30 biggest US chip stocks, at three times the daily move.

It aims for 300% of the index's move each day, then starts over.

Because SOXL resets every day, it can climb fast when chip stocks rise, as it did from the 2025 low, and fall just as hard, like the roughly 34% intraday drop seen this week. That means the fear is not always a discount: holding it for months can end far from three times the index's return in either direction.

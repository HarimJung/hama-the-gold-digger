WHY IT MATTERSBitcoin is digital money that no company or bank controls.

Only 21 million bitcoins will ever exist.

The fixed supply is the main reason holders wait through crashes, but bitcoin.org itself says the price is still very volatile and owners can unpredictably make or lose money.

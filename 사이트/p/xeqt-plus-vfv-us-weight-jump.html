ETF combosETF OVERLAP3 min readSTEP 42026-11-06

XEQT holders add VFV and pile $1,100 onto one country. Still adding?

XEQT is one fund that holds many companies at once. Its own September 2026 fact sheet says 45.79% of it is already United States companies. VFV is another fund that is 100.00% United States, so adding it moves how much of your money sits in the US — it does not add any new companies you didn't already own.
